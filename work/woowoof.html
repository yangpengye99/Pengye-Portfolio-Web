Market & Opportunity

From a growing pet economy to a new opportunity in continuous health care

As pet health needs extend from basic care to long-term health management, existing products still leave experience gaps between health monitoring, AI consultation and medical services — creating room for one integrated product that fuses smart hardware, AI and a service ecosystem.

  • Our opportunity

    Integrate monitoring, AI analysis and action guidance into one continuous health-management experience.

  • Market gap

    Health data, AI consultation and medical services are fragmented.

  • Demand shift

    From passively spotting problems to proactive, continuous health care.

  • Market foundation

    A $158 billion pet economy.

Problem

Health changes go unnoticed. AI advice lacks continuity. Anomalies are hard to act on.

Owners often miss subtle health changes. Even when they turn to AI, they have to re-describe their pet’s background and hop between tools — and when a real issue appears, they still lack clear, trustworthy next steps.

Health changes go unnoticed

Pets can’t tell us when they feel unwell. Owners rely on everyday observation with no continuous monitoring or data to compare, so early anomalies slip by.

Repetitive, tedious AI consultations

Every AI chat means re-entering pet info, symptoms and history — asking the same questions across tools with no continuous health context.

Anomalies without guidance

Facing unusual symptoms and conflicting advice, owners struggle to decide whether to see a vet — and how to observe, respond and follow up.

Competitive Analysis

From location tracking to continuous health care

Competitors leave gaps in location tracking, health-data interpretation and follow-up care. I shifted the focus from single-function monitoring to AI-driven, continuous health management — moving pet health from passive attention to proactive care.

From data display to AI health insight

From alerts to proactive care

Connects health alerts to action suggestions, ongoing observation and professional services — a complete care loop.

AI-Powered Smart Collar for Everyday Pet Health

An AI smart collar for everyday health

Once the collar is on, it continuously collects key vital signs. Current behavior, health data and AI analysis come together on the home screen, so owners see their pet’s real-time state at a glance.

Physical product design

Lightweight · Minimal · Detachable · Durable

I wanted to turn owners’ care for their pets’ health into tangible product quality. Clean, rounded forms, soft neutral colors and low-gloss translucent acrylic balance technology with warmth. The design never flaunts its tech — a restrained visual language and refined material layering convey a professional, reliable and warm character, so the collar blends naturally into daily life as a trusted health companion.
