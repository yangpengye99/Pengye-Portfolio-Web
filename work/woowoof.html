A Growing Pet Economy, a New Health-Care Opportunity

As pet health needs extend from basic care to long-term management, existing products still leave experience gaps between health monitoring, AI consultation and medical services — creating room for an integrated product that fuses smart hardware, AI and a service ecosystem.

Our Opportunity

Integrate monitoring, AI analysis and action guidance into one continuous health-management experience

Market Gap

Health data, AI consultation and medical services are fragmented

Demand Shift

Market Foundation

A $158 billion pet economy

Unnoticed Changes, Fragmented AI, Unclear Next Steps

Owners often miss subtle health changes. Even with AI they must re-describe their pet and switch tools — and when facing a potential issue, they still lack clear, trustworthy next steps.

Health Changes Go Unnoticed

Pets can’t tell us when they feel unwell. Owners rely on daily observation without continuous monitoring or data comparison, so early anomalies slip by.

Repetitive AI Consultations

Every consultation means re-entering pet info, symptoms and history, asking again across tools with no continuous health context.

Anomalies Without Guidance

Facing unusual symptoms and conflicting advice, owners struggle to decide whether to see a vet — and how to observe, respond and follow up.

From Location Tracking to Continuous Health Care

Building on competitors’ gaps in location tracking, health-data interpretation and follow-up care, I shifted the focus from single-function monitoring to AI-driven continuous health management — from passive attention to proactive care.

How do we differentiate?

From Location Tracking to Health Monitoring

Long-term wear and continuous sensing, focused on physiological and behavioural change — not just location and activity logs.

From Data Display to AI Health Insight

AI interprets continuous data, identifies anomalies and trends, and makes complex health info easy to understand.

From Alerts to Proactive Care

Connects alerts to action suggestions, ongoing observation and professional services for complete care.

A Soft, Trustworthy Visual Language

Pet health care is a long-term companion, not a quick tool. Soft colour, a light information hierarchy and an anthropomorphic pet ease the tension of medical products, while clear status feedback and risk cues keep the credibility and decision speed a health product needs.
