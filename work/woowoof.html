PROBLEM

Unnoticed Changes, Fragmented AI, Unclear Next Steps

Owners often miss subtle health changes. Even with AI they must re-describe their pet and switch tools — and when facing a potential issue, they still lack clear, trustworthy next steps.

Health Changes Go Unnoticed

Repetitive AI Consultations

Anomalies Without Guidance

Pets can’t tell us when they feel unwell. Owners rely on daily observation without continuous monitoring or data comparison, so early anomalies slip by.

Every consultation means re-entering pet info, symptoms and history, asking again across tools with no continuous health context.

Facing unusual symptoms and conflicting advice, owners struggle to decide whether to see a vet — and how to observe, respond and follow up.
