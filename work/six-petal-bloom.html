About the name

Liù chū, “six-pointed”, is the classical name for a snowflake; níng huá is deposition — vapour turning straight into crystal. “Most flowers have five petals; snowflakes alone have six.” The tool turns that line into a gesture: six taps, one crystal bloom.

Core Mechanic

Every touch shows. Every piece finishes.

Each tap reveals a share of the crackle field equal to the remaining shards ÷ remaining taps — so the sixth tap always completes the bowl. Colour is deepest at the heart and whitens toward the rim.

Four seeded results — two-layer Voronoi ice fields, each tinted by one family of neighbouring hues.

Iteration

From a mist-grey album leaf to an ink-black field

v1
  • Light mist-grey ground (#E7ECE8) washed out the crystal highlights.
  • One 1.3 MB PNG washer — no rotation, inaccurate texture mapping.
  • 6–10 crystals blurred the simple “tap six times” rule.
v2
  • Ink-black field so crystal light reads; one vermilion seal as the only accent (≤ 0.5% of the canvas).
  • Procedural 3D lathe mesh — four vessels, free rotation, accurate glaze mapping.
  • Exactly six crystals: the rule becomes the ritual.

How the crystal model evolved

  • v2.2

    Walker crack propagation — organic, but slow to read and hard to finish in six taps.

  • v2.3

    Single-centre branching tree — clearer, but every bowl looked alike.

  • v2.4

    Reveal-style Voronoi shards — each tap uncovers a wave of ice plates; finishing is guaranteed.

  • v2.5

    Seeded variety — five shard families (drift, current, bloom, plates, rift) plus mood palettes.

  • v2.6

    Clear-glaze reflection — two virtual window lights, dimmed 72% over crystals, so it stays porcelain, not glass.

Output

A poster with its own classical title

The vessel is the only hero. Two vertical columns record vessel, glaze, date and the traditional two-hour time period; a four-character seal signs it. Every work gets a generated title — “Clear Ice” and “Six-Point Album”.

Save to album or publish to RED through the native bridge; if it’s unavailable, a clean screenshot mode takes over. A local archive keeps the last eight works.

Engineering

Hand-written WebGL, fully offline

A custom lathe renderer, seeded Voronoi ice fields and a polar glaze texture — classic scripts only, under RED’s 10 MB sandbox.
