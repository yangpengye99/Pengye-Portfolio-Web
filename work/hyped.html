HYPED.

A personalized sneaker shopping app

HYPED. turns sneaker shopping from a search-driven purchase into a personalized, swipe-based discovery experience.

UI/UX DesignApp Design
CASE STUDYUX Design / Visual Design / UI Design
Problem

Too many choices leave people overwhelmed

People spend hours browsing fashion posts and hunting for styles — and hype culture keeps adding the extra chore of finding pairs they actually love.

Interviews

To understand more pain points, I interviewed sneaker buyers about how they shop today.

“Lots of platforms are too utilitarian. They’re simple, but I wish buying sneakers felt more personal.”

“Sites like SSENSE have tons of designers to browse, but I have to filter through so much I don’t care about.”

“It’s hard to find shoes that fit my taste and are practical.”

“Sometimes I just can’t find pairs in my specific style online.”

of sneaker buyers mentioned switching between platforms.

look for pairs that match their personal taste.

65%

80%

struggle to keep up with the drops that matter to them.

Pain Points

Four core user pain points

Problem 01

Recommendations ignore personal style

They understand products, not the user’s taste.

Problem 02

Constant platform switching

Scattered information keeps interrupting exploration and decisions.

Problem 03

Hard to tell if a pair suits you

Without intuitive fit cues, buying comes down to guesswork.

Problem 04

A fragmented path from discovery to purchase

Core shopping actions are disconnected — slow and incoherent.

Insights

How might we turn sneaker shopping from a scattered comparison process into a personal, delightful discovery experience?

Pain points
Buyers worry hype and limited supply mean they can’t cop the pair.
Filtering every sneaker to find favorites takes a lot of time.
Sometimes the right size is gone, or the price is higher than expected.
For limited, pricey pairs, buyers feel unsure about payment and shipping confirmation.
Opportunities
How might we help users find the sneakers they want efficiently?
How might we let users personalize how they shop?
How might we make the ideal price, size and colorway easier to find?
How might we organize info and keep buyers updated on shipping?
